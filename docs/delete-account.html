Capy Dungeon - Account and Data Deletion

Developer: Lim Wei Ye

This page explains how users of Capy Dungeon can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email limweiye@hotmail.com with subject line: Capy Dungeon Account Deletion.
  2. Include your in-game username and, if used, your social login email (Google/Facebook) to help identify your account.
  3. We may ask for a short verification reply to confirm account ownership before deletion.
Request deletion by email

What data is deleted

  • Account profile data (username, display name, social account link data).
  • Cloud gameplay data associated with that account (leaderboard profile, submitted run/stats data, equipped/stashed rings and artifacts stored in cloud).

What data may be retained

  • Transaction and payment records required for tax, fraud prevention, chargeback, and legal compliance (if applicable).
  • Minimal technical logs kept for security and abuse prevention.

Retention period

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Data that must be retained for legal or security reasons may be kept for up to 90 days, then deleted or anonymized.
